Thomas Scientific - New Model 4 Wiley® Cutting Mill

The New Model 4 Wiley® Cutting Mill by Thomas Scientific is the premier heavy-duty analytical cutting mill engineered for precise, rapid, and heat-minimized particle size reduction of fibrous, tough, and resilient materials.

Unlike conventional hammer mills or pulverizers that generate high friction and excessive thermal degradation, the Wiley® Mill utilizes four replaceable tool-steel rotating blades that shear cleanly against six stationary bed knives within an expansive 7.75-inch diameter cutting chamber. This continuous shearing action prevents thermal damage, preserving moisture and volatile analytical compounds.

Key Features & Advantages

  • Continuous Shearing Action: Minimizes heat build-up and sample degradation during milling, essential for analytical accuracy.
  • Heavy-Duty 1 HP Motor: Constant operating speed of ~920 RPM delivers high torque to process tough, resilient samples without stalling.
  • Precision Tool-Steel Cutting System: 4 revolving rotor blades and 6 stationary bed knives crafted from hardened tool steel with easily adjustable clearance.
  • Interchangeable Sieve System: Supplied with 0.5 mm, 1 mm, and 2 mm sieve meshes constructed of durable 316 Stainless Steel.
  • Safe & Controlled Sample Feeding: Heavy-duty 300-series stainless steel hopper equipped with removable safety insert and plunger.
  • Convenient Sample Collection: Threaded jar adapter securely accommodates standard 16 oz glass collection jars for direct sample reception.

Standard Applications

  • Agriculture & Feed: Animal feed, cereal grains, forage, hay, straw, corn, soybeans, seeds, tobacco, and dried plant tissue.
  • Biomass & Biofuels: Bagasse, switchgrass, wood chips, bark, cellulose, and forestry by-products.
  • Plastics & Polymers: Polyethylene, polypropylene, synthetic fibers, elastomer pellets, and recycled polymers.
  • Food & Pharmaceuticals: Dehydrated food samples, roots, herbs, botanical extracts, spices, and tablet excipients.
  • Industrial Materials: Cardboard, paper pulp, leather, rubber, and solid waste materials.
